# Page Outlines

## Status and Scope

These are structural outlines for approved public pages. They are not polished final website copy, legal terms, regulatory verification, design files, implementation instructions, or a current-site audit.

All legal and policy pages are structural draft outlines requiring final legal and business review. Regulatory claims must be verified against current official DOT or applicable DOT-agency sources before publication.

## Global Content Rules for All Pages

- Keep the tone calm, practical, direct, respectful, and worker-facing.
- Do not promise job placement, return to duty, continued employment, licensing, reinstatement, a SAP outcome, legal advice, medical advice, or guaranteed compliance.
- Clearly distinguish FMCSA-specific content from broader DOT content.
- Keep material eligibility rules, limitations, and waiting periods visible.
- Do not present unresolved business, legal, operational, payment, cancellation, provider, or benefit details as final.
- Mark claims requiring regulatory verification before publication.
