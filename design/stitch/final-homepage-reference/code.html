Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible for the program? expand_more
CDL drivers and other FMCSA-covered professionals may join before a DOT drug or alcohol violation or refusal occurs. Return-to-duty benefits become available after 90 consecutive days of active membership, subject to eligibility requirements and final membership terms.
Does this pay for my drug tests? expand_more
The program covers practice tests and RTD educational materials. Costs for official employer-mandated tests or legal fees are not included.
Is my information confidential? expand_more
Absolutely. Your membership and any inquiries you make are strictly confidential and are not reported to your employer or any federal databases.
